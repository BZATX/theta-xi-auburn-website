Welcome, Parents

A Safe, Supportive Home

Joining a fraternity is a big decision — we get that. Our goal is straightforward: give your son a safe, supportive place to grow, in and out of the classroom.

The chapter puts academics, leadership, and service first, and the brothers hold each other accountable — we expect them to carry themselves like gentlemen.

The chapter house — your son's home away from home.
Our Commitment

What Your Son Gains

  • Academic support: study hours and academic mentorship to ensure our brothers succeed in the classroom.
  • Scholarships: up to $22,500 per year in merit-based and need-based awards for new members — one of the largest scholarship endowments of any Theta Xi chapter nationwide. See the scholarships →
  • Leadership opportunities: from executive board positions to committee leadership, numerous ways to develop essential skills.
  • Philanthropy and service: active community service and fundraising that gives back to the Auburn community.
  • A network for life: an extensive alumni network eager to mentor active brothers with career guidance and enduring friendships.
