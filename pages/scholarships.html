Rising Stars Scholarships

Merit-Based · Up to 10 Per Year

Awarded to up to five new Theta Xi Associate Members each semester in recognition of leadership and academic excellence. Each recipient in good standing receives a total award of $3,250 across three semesters.

Dr. S. Allen Edgar Scholarship

Need-Based · Up to 1 Per Year

Awarded to one new Auburn Theta Xi member per year, designated for individuals who may not traditionally have been able to afford the Theta Xi fraternity experience. The recipient in good standing receives a total award of $9,500 across four years.
