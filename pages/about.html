Who We Are

What is Theta Xi?

Theta Xi is a college home for its brothers at Auburn. Between academics, service, and social events, there's plenty of room to grow and step into leadership.

Founded at Auburn in 1954, the Beta Zeta chapter has been a home for generations of undergraduates and alumni over the past seven decades.

The Beta Zeta chapter house — 835 W Magnolia Ave.
National Recognition

An All True Men Chapter

Theta Xi Headquarters established the All True Men program to recognize and support chapters that go above and beyond the standards for accreditation. We are proud to be an officially designated All True Men chapter, recognized for:

  • Excellence in academic achievement
  • Dedication to community service and philanthropic pursuits
  • Exceptional participation in campus organizations
  • Strong alumni engagement and professional development
Our Mission

Building Men of Character

The mission of the Beta Zeta chapter is to build not just good students, but good men who go out into the world and make a tangible difference in their communities.

Our brothers are NASA astronauts, FBI operatives, leaders of industry, and experts in their fields. Your time as an undergraduate equips you with the tools to succeed in life and propel your career in ways non-Greek students simply won't.
