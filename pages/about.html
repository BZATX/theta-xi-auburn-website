Who We Are

What is Theta Xi?

Theta Xi is a leadership and social fraternity at Auburn. Brothers study together, do service work together, and run the chapter themselves.

Beta Zeta was founded at Auburn in 1954, and generations of brothers have come through the house since.

The Beta Zeta chapter house at 835 W Magnolia Ave.
National Recognition

An All True Men Chapter

Theta Xi's national headquarters gives the All True Men title to chapters that go beyond its accreditation standards. Beta Zeta is an All True Men chapter, recognized for:

  • Strong grades
  • Community service and fundraising
  • Involvement in campus organizations
  • Alumni involvement and career development
Our Mission

Building Men of Character

We want our brothers to leave Auburn as good students and good men who show up for their communities.

Beta Zeta alumni include a NASA astronaut, a former FBI counterintelligence operative, a former director of the Defense Intelligence Agency, and the CEO of Great Southern Wood Preserving.
