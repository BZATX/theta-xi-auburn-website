The Chapter Still Needs You

Beta Zeta runs on alumni who stay engaged. If you have time to offer, there's a seat for you:

  • Mentor–Mentee Program — pair with an undergraduate and help him get ready for life after Auburn.
  • Assistant Chapter Advisor (local alumni) — support and guide the active chapter alongside the Chapter Advisor.
  • Life Skills & Leadership Committee — help plan programs on practical development, careers, and personal growth.
  • Show up — game-day tailgates at the house, Homecoming, and alumni events. Sometimes that's the most valuable thing.
