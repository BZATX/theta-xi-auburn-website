The Official Side

How Auburn IFC Recruitment Works

Theta Xi is a member of Auburn's Interfraternity Council, which sets the university-wide recruitment calendar. Joining any IFC fraternity involves a few official steps alongside getting to know the chapter:

  • Register with IFC. All men rushing an IFC fraternity register through ChapterBuilder. Registration opens May 1 and is free.
  • Rushing costs nothing. There is no fee to participate in recruitment. If you accept a bid, a one-time $70 IFC bidding fee is charged to your Auburn student eBill.
  • Eligibility. You must be a full-time enrolled Auburn undergraduate to join a fraternity.
  • GPA requirements are set chapter by chapter — talk to our Recruitment Chairman about where you stand.

Dates and details are set by Auburn IFC each year — confirm the current calendar at Auburn IFC's Join page.

IFC recruitment registration opens May 1 on ChapterBuilder. Sign up early — it puts you on every chapter's radar.

Summer

Summer Rush

The primary season. Chapters host relaxed events all summer so potential new members and brothers have real time to get to know each other. Most men who join Beta Zeta join this way.

Fall

Formal Recruitment Week

Early in the fall semester, IFC hosts events like Rec Day, Open House, and Kickoff where you can meet every fraternity, with formal recruitment typically the second week of classes.

Year-Round

Open Bidding

Chapters can extend bids outside the formal periods through continuous open bidding — which is why rush at Beta Zeta never really closes. Reach out whenever you're ready.

Common Questions

Rush FAQ

When can I rush?

Any time. Summer and the start of fall semester are the busiest seasons, but Beta Zeta recruits year-round through open bidding. Spring works too.

Does rushing cost anything?

No — rushing is completely free. The only cost in the process is a one-time $70 IFC fee billed by Auburn if you accept a bid.

Do I need an invitation or recommendation?

No. You don't need to know a brother, a legacy, or anyone in Greek life. Reaching out is all it takes — most of our brothers started exactly that way.

What is the chapter looking for?

Men of integrity who can carry our four pillars — scholarship, brotherhood, service, and leadership. Come as you are; character matters more than résumé.

What about grades?

Academics come first here — study hours, tutoring, and up to $22,500 a year in scholarships back that up. GPA expectations are chapter-set, so ask our Recruitment Chairman where you stand.

What happens after I accept a bid?

You'll join as an Associate Member and go through new member education with your Associate Member Class — and you're immediately eligible to apply for the Rising Stars and Edgar scholarships.
