Juncti Juvant

Philanthropy and Service

Our motto, Juncti Juvant, translates to "United They Serve" — and every fall we back it up with a sledgehammer.

Our Signature Event

The Taxi Bash

The Taxi Bash is our signature philanthropy event, where an old car is hauled out to the chapter house, painted by the brothers, and then beaten to pieces. The funds collected from participants — who pay for swings to destroy the Taxi Bash car — are donated to the National MS Society.

Where It Goes

The Causes We Serve

National Beneficiary

National MS Society

The MS Society is Theta Xi's national philanthropy. Beyond the Taxi Bash, the brothers turn out for MS Walks and run fundraisers through the year — all to help end multiple sclerosis for good.
